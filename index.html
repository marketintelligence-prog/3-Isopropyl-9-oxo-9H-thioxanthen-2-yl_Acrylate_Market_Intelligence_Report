Executive Summary

What it is. 3-Isopropyl-9-oxo-9H-thioxanthen-2-yl acrylate is the acrylate ester of 2-hydroxy-3-isopropylthioxanthone. It joins the light-absorbing core of ITX, the most widely used Type II photoinitiator, to an acrylate group. The acrylate copolymerises into the cured ink or coating, so the initiator cannot migrate out. This is the design principle behind the industry's "polymerizable thioxanthone" class (Agfa patents US9278949, US8530510).

Market reality. We found no CAS number, PubChem entry, catalogue listing or commercial trade name for this exact molecule. It should be treated as a new or custom molecule: a patent candidate, a customer's development compound, or a research target. It competes with established low-migration thioxanthones: IGM Omnipol TX, Rahn Genopol TX-2 and Arkema/Lambson SpeedCure 7010 (CAS 1003567-83-6). Those products already serve the regulatory pull away from ITX in food packaging. ITX is self-classified Repr. 2 (H361f) and Aquatic Chronic 1, and it has had food-contamination incidents since 2005.

Why now. Three regulatory changes and one deal push demand toward non-migrating photoinitiators. The EU PPWR has applied since 12-Aug-2026. The German Printing Ink Ordinance positive list applies from 1-Jan-2027. India's FSSAI requires IS 15495-compliant food-packaging inks. And iGM Resins, the largest Western photoinitiator maker, is being sold to China's Jiuri-led consortium (signed 10-Nov-2026), which will concentrate global supply in China.

Product Overview & Identity

Introduction

Thioxanthones absorb strongly in the near-UV (ITX maxima at 259 and 383 nm Arkema). They are the preferred sensitisers for 365–395 nm UV-LED curing. Small-molecule ITX migrates out of cured films, however. In 2005 ITX was found in infant milk packed in printed cartons (Wikipedia, BfR). Since then the industry has moved to polymeric and polymerizable thioxanthones. The target molecule attaches an acrylate directly to the phenolic 2-position of a 3-isopropyl thioxanthone core. That gives a compact, low-viscosity, copolymerizable initiator, the profile Agfa describes as needed for low-viscosity, LED-sensitive, low-migration inkjet inks (US9278949).

Regiochemistry check. 2,3-Dimethylphenol + thiosalicylic acid gives 2-hydroxy-3,4-dimethylthioxanthone (US4418138). The phenol OH ends up at C-2 and the ortho substituent at C-3. By the same logic, 2-isopropylphenol should give the required 2-hydroxy-3-isopropyl core. Expect a minor isomer from ring closure at the hindered position. The 1,3-dimethyl analogue from 2,6-dimethylphenol follows the same chemistry (US9500947).

Industry Applications, End-Products & Market Sizes

A. Low-migration UV / UV-LED printing inks (food & pharma packaging) — PRIMARY

This is the main use case. Polymerizable thioxanthones are built for inks that need low viscosity, low migration after cure and high LED sensitivity at the same time (US9278949). The acrylate lets the initiator lock into the network. Commercial polymeric TX products make the same claims. SpeedCure 7010 showed undetected migratable TX in GC/MS extraction, versus detectable TX for ITX (Arkema flyer).

B. UV / LED-curable coatings & adhesives

This includes wood, plastic and metal coatings, nail gels, and pressure-sensitive and laminating adhesives. Thioxanthones give depth cure in pigmented (TiO2) systems. SpeedCure 7010 lists industrial coatings, nail care, 3D printing and labels among its applications (Arkema). The low-odour, non-migrating benefit is valued in nail care (skin contact) and in interior coatings.

C. 3D printing, dental and biomedical photopolymers

One-component, polymerizable TX initiators have been proposed for food-packaging and biomedical uses because of their migration stability (RSC Adv. 2016, Polymers 2025). Vat-photopolymerisation (405 nm DLP/LCD) resins and dental composites are adjacent, higher-margin niches. Volumes are small (kg to low tonnes).

D. Cationic sensitisation & photoresists (electronics)

Thioxanthones sensitise iodonium and sulfonium salts so that cationic epoxy systems can cure at near-UV or LED wavelengths (Politecnico di Torino; SpeedCure 7010 is marketed as a sensitiser for LED cationic formulations Arkema). Thioxanthone derivatives are also patented for photoresist compositions (US12398152).

In photoresists the role is indirect: a sensitiser or PAG chromophore, not a bulk component. The acrylate function is useful only in negative-tone acrylic resists, solder masks and dry films, not in chemically-amplified positive resists.

E. Research & reference standards

Migration-testing labs need reference standards for target analytes and their photoproducts. ITX is already sold as a PESTANAL analytical standard for food-packaging residue testing (Sigma-Aldrich). If the target molecule is commercialised, a matching standard (plus its 2-OH-ITX hydrolysis product) will be needed for migration-compliance testing.

Not applicable: pharmaceutical intermediates, agrochemicals, food, flavour or fragrance, and construction. We found no use of this molecule in these clusters.

Feasibility Summary & Management Recommendation

VERDICT: CONDITIONAL — Enter via CRO / custom synthesis; gate manufacturing on a committed customer

The molecule is technically easy for us and fits a real regulatory trend (low-migration UV-LED inks). But it is not a commercial product today: it has no CAS, no supplier and no visible buyer, and qualified polymeric thioxanthones already fill the need. The best value now is (1) responding to whoever is asking for it with fast gram-to-kg custom synthesis, (2) building a 2-hydroxythioxanthone platform that also supports ITX/DETX import substitution, and (3) holding manufacturing capex until a sponsor commits volume of at least 5 t/yr and regulatory funding (REACH/TSCA/GIO).
